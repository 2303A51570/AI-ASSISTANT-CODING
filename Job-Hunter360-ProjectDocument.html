Module 2 — Smart Matching

AI job matching engine

The system searches the web for real job openings matching your title, then Claude AI scores each one against your resume. Jobs below 60% are skipped automatically.

FAQ
Common questions
Will employers know my application was automated? ▾
No. Each email is written uniquely by Claude AI for that specific company and role. It reads like a human-written cover letter, includes your real name and experience, and is sent from your own Gmail address. Employers see a normal professional email.
What if the AI match score is wrong? ▾
You can lower or raise the match threshold in the backend (.env or code). The default is 60%. You can also manually add companies via the Companies page and use the Manual Apply button to send to those regardless of score.
Is the job search actually finding real jobs? ▾
Yes. The system uses Claude AI's web search capability to find real, current job postings from company websites and job boards. It searches the live web, not a cached database. Results will vary based on what's available at the time of search.
What does it cost to run? ▾
The hosting on Render.com is free for the starter tier. Gmail SMTP is free. The only cost is the Anthropic API — roughly $0.003 per resume parse and $0.01–0.02 per full smart apply run (searching, matching, composing). Very affordable for regular use.
Can I use a resume that isn't in English? ▾
Claude AI supports multiple languages, so it can parse resumes in other languages. However, the job search and email composition will work best if your resume and target jobs are in the same language.
How do I update my resume? ▾
Go to the Resume page and click "Replace". Upload your new PDF. The AI will re-parse it and update your profile. All future match scoring and email writing will use the new version.
